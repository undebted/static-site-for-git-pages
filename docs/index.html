What is undebted.?

Each UNDBT token represents $1 of verified U.S. federal student loan debt, creating a new asset class that empowers borrowers and enables community support. Verify your debt and we give you the tokens, that's it.

Verify & Mint

Complete a secure credit check to verify your student loan debt and mint UNDBT tokens representing your education investment.

Community Support

Donors can burn UNDBT tokens to directly support borrowers, creating a decentralized student debt relief system.

Education Assets

Transform educational debt into tradeable assets, creating new financial opportunities and debt management strategies.

Security First

Do This:
  • Write down your seed phrase offline
  • Store it in a secure location
  • Never share it with anyone
  • Use strong passwords
Never Do This:
  • Share your private keys
  • Store seed phrases digitally
  • Click suspicious links
  • Connect to unknown websites

Who We Are

Graham and Jon are two smart mofos, just like you. Unfortunately (also just like you) nobody told us our education loans were going to screw us for the rest of our lives.

This is a non-profit project aimed at helping us all get out from under the heel of a predatory loan system. We don't benefit any more than anyone else who joins up will benefit.

We are all in this together, let's turn this around and show them what we're made of.
